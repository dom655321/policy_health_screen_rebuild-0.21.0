RESOLUTION NO. [____]

A RESOLUTION OF THE [GOVERNING BODY] OF {{ entity|upper }} REVIEWING THE INVESTMENT POLICY AND INVESTMENT STRATEGIES AND ADOPTING AMENDMENTS THERETO

WHEREAS, Chapter 2256, Texas Government Code (the "Public Funds Investment Act"), requires the [Governing Body] to adopt by rule, order, ordinance or resolution a written investment policy and investment strategies; and

WHEREAS, Section 2256.005(e) of the Act requires the [Governing Body] to review its investment policy and investment strategies not less than annually and to adopt a written instrument stating that it has done so and recording any changes made to either; and

WHEREAS, the [Governing Body] has reviewed the Investment Policy and Investment Strategies and the proposed amendments set out in Exhibit A;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE [GOVERNING BODY] OF {{ entity|upper }}:

Section 1. The [Governing Body] has reviewed the Investment Policy and Investment Strategies as required by Section 2256.005(e) of the Act.

Section 2. The amendments set out in Exhibit A are adopted, and the Investment Policy as so amended is the investment policy of {{ entity }}.

Section 3. This resolution takes effect on [date].
