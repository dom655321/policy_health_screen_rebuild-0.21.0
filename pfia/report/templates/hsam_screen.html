Basis and limits of this screen
This screen compares the adopted investment policy against a machine-readable register of Tex. Gov't Code ch. 2256, Subchapter A. Every finding rests on a passage located and verified in the document itself. · It reads the policy document only. It does not examine the entity's investment practices, holdings or management controls, and it is not an audit. · Sections of the subchapter not covered by the register are not examined, and their absence from these observations says nothing about them either way. · A policy more restrictive than the chapter is conforming: Sec. 2256.024 makes the subchapter cumulative, and stricter internal limits are recorded as such. · Observations are generated by software, have not been reviewed by a licensed professional, and do not constitute legal advice or a determination of compliance. {% if client_safe.coverage_statement %} · {{ client_safe.coverage_statement }} {% endif %}

How to work this document
Start with the reviewer work queue. Each finding names the kind of check it needs, and those are different jobs: confirming an absence is not the same as reading a flagged passage, and neither is the same as a judgment call on contested text. Resolve each item as control wrong, reviewer wrong or evidence missed — only the first changes the register.
