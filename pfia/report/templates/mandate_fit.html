Reading the fit

Blocks the strategy uses an instrument the policy does not authorize. Constrains the policy's binding term is narrower than the strategy's. Fits the strategy runs as designed. Cannot determine the review could not read the policy's term with certainty. Not used the policy permits something the strategy does not use. The binding term is the stricter of the policy and the Act; where the policy is silent or conflicts, the Act's term applies.

What this is not

An internal onboarding aid built from review ledger {{ ledger_id }} (package {{ package_version }}). It is not a compliance opinion, it is not sent to the client, and it is not loaded into a trading or compliance system.
