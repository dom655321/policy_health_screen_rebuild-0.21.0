Basis and limits

The review compares the adopted investment policy with a register of Tex. Gov't Code ch. 2256, Subchapter A, focused on authorized investments and investment management provisions. Every observation rests on a passage located and verified in the document. It reads the policy only: it does not examine practices, holdings or controls, and it is not an audit, a determination of compliance or legal advice. A policy more restrictive than the Act conforms (Sec. 2256.024). Ch. 2257 collateral is outside its scope.
